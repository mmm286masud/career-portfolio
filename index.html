Research Question

What technical skills and professional experience do employers expect from AI Software Engineers, and how does this compare with Forward Deployed Engineering?

This research is intended for technical recruiters, hiring managers, and people exploring careers in artificial intelligence.

Research date: November 23, 2026

Employer Research

Three AI-related job descriptions were selected for comparison.

OpenAI — Forward Deployed Engineer

Focuses on building and deploying production AI applications while collaborating directly with customers.

Skills: Software engineering, Python, JavaScript, AI integration, and technical communication.

Original OpenAI Job Description

Gamma — AI Engineer

Focuses on improving AI-powered software features, evaluating AI models, and building reliable AI product experiences.

Skills: Python, TypeScript, AI evaluation, and software development.

Original Gamma Job Description

Optum — Associate AI/ML Engineer

Focuses on AI applications in healthcare, conversational AI, cloud development, and security.

Skills: Software engineering, AWS, AI/ML integration, and cloud services.

Original Optum Job Description

Career Direction Comparison

AI Software Engineering

AI Software Engineers develop applications that integrate AI models, evaluate their behavior, and deliver reliable software.

Forward Deployed Engineering

Forward Deployed Engineers work directly with organizations to develop, integrate, and deploy technical solutions.

My preferred direction: AI Software Engineering because I am interested in building AI-powered applications.

Portfolio Design Decisions

I compared two design approaches: Clean & Professional and Modern Developer.

Selected: Clean & Professional.

The goal is to make technical projects, skills, and experience easy for recruiters and hiring managers to review.

My future portfolio will include Home, About, Projects, Skills, and Contact sections.
